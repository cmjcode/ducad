DuCAD Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: September 2026 • Platform: macOS, iPadOS, iOS, Windows, Linux

Core Privacy Summary (Zero Data Collection)

  • 100% Offline & Local-First: All 3D models, 2D sketches, parametric history, and drawing sheets stay on your device.
  • Zero Personal Data Collection: We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, IP addresses, or location data.
  • No Tracking & No Ads: No third-party analytics SDKs, behavioral trackers, or ad networks within the application.
  • No Account Required: DuCAD is ready to use immediately without registration, sign-in, or mandatory third-party cloud.
  • Complete User Control: You maintain 100% ownership and control over your files and project data.

1. Introduction

DuCAD ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by PT. VNEU TEKNOLOGI INDONESIA, is fully committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the DuCAD application across all supported platforms, including macOS and iPadOS on the Apple App Store.

Our guiding principle is simple: DuCAD is architected from the ground up as a pure offline-first application. We do not collect, store on remote servers, process, or sell your personal data.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike conventional cloud-centric platforms, DuCAD operates entirely on your local machine. Specifically:

  • Personal Identifiable Information: We do not collect your name, street address, email address, phone number, or national identifiers.
  • User Accounts: No account creation, password, or login credentials are required to use DuCAD.
  • Telemetry & Usage Analytics: We do not track which features you click, how frequently you open the application, or the duration of your modeling sessions.
  • Sensitive Device Information: We do not collect hardware serial numbers, IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers), or biometric data.
  • Third-Party Trackers & Advertisers: DuCAD contains zero advertising SDKs, tracking libraries (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or Facebook SDK), or data brokers.

3. Storage of Your CAD Designs & Files

All CAD design data — including 2D sketches, 3D solid B-Rep geometry, assemblies, ISO technical drawings, and export files (such as STEP, STL, OBJ, DXF, SVG, PDF, and GLTF) — are generated, calculated by the OpenCASCADE kernel, and saved exclusively on your local device storage.

We have no access to your project files, intellectual property, or design geometry. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to transfer, share, or export it yourself.

4. iCloud & User-Managed Storage

On macOS and iPadOS, you may optionally choose to store or open project files using Apple iCloud Drive or other file providers. When you utilize iCloud, file synchronization and data encryption are managed directly by Apple in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy. DuCAD does not operate any intermediate cloud servers and has no access to your Apple ID credentials.

5. Device Permissions

DuCAD adheres to the principle of least privilege and strict Apple App Sandbox requirements:

  • Files & Document Access: Requested only when you open, save, or export files through the system Document Picker. DuCAD only accesses the specific files or folders you explicitly select.
  • Graphics Acceleration (GPU / WebGPU / Metal): Utilized strictly on-device to render 3D scenes and the user interface at high frame rates. No rendered visual frames or canvas buffers are ever captured or transmitted externally.
  • No Camera, Microphone, Contacts, or Location Access: DuCAD never requests or accesses your camera, microphone, contact address book, or precise GPS location.

6. Third-Party Services & External Links

DuCAD does not include third-party advertising or analytics services. When you click external links within the application or documentation (such as our GitHub repository at github.com/cmjcode/ducad or open-source licenses), your default system web browser will navigate to those external domains, which operate under their own respective privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

DuCAD does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction). DuCAD is completely safe for use in educational institutions, engineering schools, universities, and by young designers and students.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not collect or store your personal information on our servers, there is no personal data held by us to retain or delete. You retain 100% control over all files on your device: deleting a project file from your local storage or iCloud Drive deletes it permanently and immediately.

9. User Rights (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, & UU PDP)

Under global data protection regulations (including the EU GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, and Indonesian Personal Data Protection Act / UU PDP), individuals hold rights regarding access, deletion, and restriction of personal data. Because DuCAD does not collect, store, or process any personal data, your privacy is safeguarded by default (privacy by design).

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect application enhancements or regulatory changes. Any updates will be published immediately on this page (https://ducad.app/PrivacyPolicy.html) with a revised "Last Updated" date.
